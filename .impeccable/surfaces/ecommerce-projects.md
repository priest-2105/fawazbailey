# Ecommerce projects

Route: `/ecommerce-projects`
Mode: Experience
Scope: This route only; the original portfolio keeps its comic identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A storefront collection where actual shop screens lead and each project tells the story of its particular shopping journey.

OWN-WORLD: Pale cool green, deep pine text, quiet rules, large Manrope headings, generous consistent gutters. Rectangular website captures sit in broad green or lilac fields; no comic borders or floating decorations.

STORY: Visitors see the stores, choose a project, read its context and build decisions, inspect desktop/mobile captures, then visit the store or contact Fawaz.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Small independent masthead; large left-aligned two-line title with a compact introduction on the right; two actual storefront previews directly underneath. Primary action is the linked screenshot leading to the story.

FORM: Spacious gallery with an editorial project index and full-width project studies. Direct implementation for the user's speed constraint; no standing workflow preference inferred from the unanswered optional question.

INTERACTION: Each project has accessible Desktop/Mobile screenshot tabs; mobile images retain their natural phone proportions. Quiet transitions respect reduced motion. In-page anchors provide direct access to stories.

EXTENSION: Both stories explain the Shopify backend / Next.js frontend split. Augusta documents newsletter eligibility and automatic discounts with no CRO claim. 920 explains its Shopify deposit checkout and calendar webhook, with a responsive Recharts weekly-results chart. Reported approximate counts are not a funnel or a time series; the appointment range remains 15–20 and details expose measurement limitations.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
